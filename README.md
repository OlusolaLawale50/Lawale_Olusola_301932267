# Public Health Vaccination Programme

Olusola Lawale · Student ID 301932267

## Selected case study

**Public Health Vaccination Programme** is a fictional system for recording vaccinations and reviewing programme activity. Clinic staff record vaccinations, and programme analysts review and summarise the records. PostgreSQL stores patient identities and birth dates, clinic names and locations, vaccine products and manufacturers, and vaccination dates and dose numbers linked to those records. MongoDB holds example vaccination events with embedded snapshots of related details.

The system supports recording a vaccination, retrieving its patient, clinic and vaccine details, and summarising activity by clinic and vaccine. The five portfolios explore its relational design, queries and indexes, transactions, document model, and a proposed distributed deployment.
